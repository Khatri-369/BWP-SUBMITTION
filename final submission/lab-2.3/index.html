Welcome to The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda

The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda (MSU), located in the cultural capital of Gujarat – Vadodara, is a premier state university with a unique residential and unitary character. Established in 1949 under the vision of Maharaja Pratapsinhrao Gaekwad and named in honor of his visionary grandfather, Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad III, the university is rooted in educational innovation and national leadership.

MSU offers the only English-medium curriculum among Gujarat state universities, spanning comprehensive academic programs in Engineering & Technology, Fine Arts, Performing Arts, Science, Commerce, Medicine, Law, Social Work, and Management Studies.

With its rich architectural heritage, extensive green campus, advanced research laboratories, and distinguished alumni worldwide, MSU Baroda stands as a beacon of academic excellence in India.

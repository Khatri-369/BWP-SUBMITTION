About the Faculty

The Faculty of Technology and Engineering (FTE), popularly known as Kala Bhavan, was established in June 1890 by His Highness Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad III of Baroda State. It is one of India's oldest and premier engineering institutions. When The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda was founded in 1949, the historic Kala Bhavan Technical Institute became the Faculty of Technology and Engineering.

FTE offers undergraduate (B.E.), postgraduate (M.E., MCA, M.Sc.), and doctoral (Ph.D.) degrees in modern engineering disciplines. With state-of-the-art laboratories, renowned faculty, extensive industry tie-ups, and an illustrious alumni base across the globe, FTE continues to set benchmarks in technical education and research.
